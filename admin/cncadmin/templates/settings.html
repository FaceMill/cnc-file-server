Before you switch it on:

  • The Pi leaves its current Wi-Fi network. This page, SSH, Raspberry Pi Connect and the office share stop working from the office network until the hotspot is off. Only devices on the hotspot reach the Pi.
  • No internet while it's on: no updates, no alert emails, and the clock isn't synchronised.
  • The CNC network keeps working: machines can still read and save files.
  • After switching, join the hotspot, open https://10.42.0.1:8443 and click Confirm within {{ hotspot_confirm // 60 }} minutes. If you don't, the Pi goes back to its previous Wi-Fi network by itself.
  • If it doesn't start, the Wi-Fi country is probably not set (sudo raspi-config → Localisation Options → WLAN Country). The Pi goes back to its previous Wi-Fi network.
  • Anyone with the password can reach the login page and the office share: share it only with the people setting up. Switch the hotspot off before the Pi goes into service; it is a Wi-Fi network your IT department hasn't approved.

Alert emails

Every 5 minutes the server checks services, disk space, backups, serial ports and the Pi itself (temperature, power, SSD wear, clock, UPS). New and resolved problems are listed on the Status page and, if turned on here, emailed. Critical problems are re-sent once a day until fixed.
